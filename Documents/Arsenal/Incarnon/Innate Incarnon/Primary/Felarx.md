> Dethroned Tigris Prime as the King of Shotguns for single target damage.
> Dethroned Arca Plasmor and pre-nerf Catchmoon as the King of Shotguns for range and infinite punch through.

# Strengths
Where do I begin?
- **Devastating Attrition**
	- 50% chance to deal +2000% damage on *[[#^9e123e|non-critical]]* hits.
- High damage. **This shotgun does not shoot marshmallow buckshots.**
- High fire rate.
- Innate punch through.
- Multiplicative damage with [[Galvanized Savvy]].
- Shell by shell reload.
	- In case you need a one shot more to finish an enemy off.
# Weaknesses
- Devastating Attrition's Requirements
	- However, this is also one of its biggest weaknesses as you usually run into squads who increase Critical Chance for *everyone* in the squad which is undesired in the case of Devastating Attrition. ^9e123e
- Also shell by shell reload...
	- Shell by shell reload to reload the entire magazine (especially with a larger magazine) will feel slower to reach maximum capacity.
