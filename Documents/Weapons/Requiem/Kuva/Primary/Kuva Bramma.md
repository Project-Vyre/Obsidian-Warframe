> A bow that launches arrows with explosive payloads. Used to be good prior to AoE ammo nerfs. Current ranking is highly dependent on if you use [[Primary Compression]] combined with Primed Sniper Ammo Mutation.
> 
> Steel Path usability **without** [[Primary Compression]] and [[Primed Sniper Ammo Mutation]]: **F**
> Normal usability **without** [[Primary Compression]] and [[Primed Sniper Ammo Mutation]]: **D**
> 
> Steel Path usability **with** [[Primary Compression]] and [[Primed Sniper Ammo Mutation]]: A
> Normal usability **with** [[Primary Compression]] and [[Primed Sniper Ammo Mutation]]: A+
