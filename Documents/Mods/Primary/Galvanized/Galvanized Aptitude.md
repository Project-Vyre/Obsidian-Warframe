# Rank 10
- +80% Status Chance
- On Kill:
    - +40% Direct Damage per **unique** Status Type affecting the target for 20s.
    - Stacks up to 2x.

> [!FAILURE] A big asterisk...
> The added damage **does not** apply to Radial Damage.

> [!TIP] Tip
> Often used in [[Status Overload]] builds.
