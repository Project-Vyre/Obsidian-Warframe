On Headshot:
- 120% Critical Chance for 12s.
On Headshot Kill:
- +40% Critical Chance when Aiming for 12s.
- Stacks up to 5x.

> [!WARNING]
> When an enemy dies from DoT (Damage over Time) on a Headshot, it is not considered a Headshot Kill and as such will not contribute a stack.
