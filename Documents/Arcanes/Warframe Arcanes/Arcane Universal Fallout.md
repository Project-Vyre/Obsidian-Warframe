# Rank 5
Each Radiation Status Effect inflicted on enemies by Abilities gives a 6% chance to drop a Universal Orb on enemy death.
+1 Arcane Revive

> [!TIP] Tip
> Only used on Warframes or with subsumed abilities that are capable of inflicting Radiation Status Effects. This includes Oberon, Qorvex, Nyx, Mesa with Radiation modded Regulators (also includes Warframes with Exalted abilities that accept elemental mods to make Radiation Status Effects) and Lavos.
> 
> While it can technically work on Lavos, it's a bit redundant due to his 3rd ability already converting all Health and Energy orbs into Universal orbs.

> [!WARNING]
> On Dante, his Noctua's mods applied to Allies **inherits** the mods, meaning free Radiation Status Effects for **everyone** using weapons. 
> However, the Status Chance to Fire Rate ratio is not exactly in its favor to apply 10 stacks of Radiation in a timely manner. While it does technically work, it should be supplemented with Khra Canticle to drop Universal Orbs at a 12% Drop Rate.
