# Rank 5
Each Radiation Status Effect inflicted on enemies by Abilities gives a 6% chance to drop a Universal Orb on enemy death.
+1 Arcane Revive

> [!TIP] Recommendation
> Arcane Universal Fallout should only used based on the following criteria:
> - Warframes with abilities that are able to inflict Radiation innately. This includes Oberon, Qorvex, Nyx, Lavos and Warframes with Exalted weapons modded for Radiation status that have a **usable** Status Chance.
> - Warframes with **subsumed abilities** that are capable of inflicting Radiation Status Effects.
> 
> While it can technically work on Lavos, it's a bit redundant due to his 3rd ability already converting all Health and Energy orbs into Universal orbs with no extra steps. If you want even more Universal Orbs... then be my guest.

> [!WARNING]
> On Dante, his Noctua's mods are applied to Allies **inherits** the mods, meaning free Radiation Status Effects for **everyone** using weapons.
> However, the Status Chance to Fire Rate ratio is not exactly in its favor to apply 10 stacks of Radiation in a timely manner. While it does technically work, it should be supplemented with Khra Canticle to drop Universal Orbs at a 12% Drop Rate.
