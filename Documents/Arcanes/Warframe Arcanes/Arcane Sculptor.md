# Rank 5
- On creating an object with abilities:
- Ability Casts have 175% Ability Efficiency for 12s.

> [!WARNING] Disclaimer
> **Sets** your Ability Strength to 175%, thus overriding all of your Ability Efficiency mods and freeing up mod slots.
> 
> Further Ability Efficiency can be added with additional Ability Duration.
