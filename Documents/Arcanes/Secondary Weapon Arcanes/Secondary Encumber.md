# Rank 5
On Status Effect:
+24% chance to trigger a second random Status Effect.

> [!TIP] Tip
> Should be used with weapons and builds with high Status Chance. However, it must be noted that the Arcane's effect has a refresh cooldown. Ties into [[Status Overload]] builds.
