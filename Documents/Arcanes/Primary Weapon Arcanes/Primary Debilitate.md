# Rank 5
If an enemy has 10 stacks of a combined Status Effect, inflicting the same Status Effect again has a 100% chance to inflict one of the base Status Effects it is composed of.

> [!TIP] Tip
> Should be used with weapons that have a high Status Chance in combination with [[Galvanized Aptitude]] and [[Galvanized Savvy]]. High fire rate is desired but not necessary if the weapon can apply many stacks as in a short amount of time due to having over 100% Status Chance.

To break down how this Arcane works, here's an example.

Let's say you reach 10 stacks of [[Viral]] on an enemy. On the 11th application of Viral status you will inflict either [[Toxin]] or [[Cold]] which in turns results in a total of 3 **unique** status effects. Combined with the **Galvanized Condition Overload** mods [[Galvanized Aptitude]] for Rifles and [[Galvanized Savvy]] for Shotguns you're able to increase your damage even further.
