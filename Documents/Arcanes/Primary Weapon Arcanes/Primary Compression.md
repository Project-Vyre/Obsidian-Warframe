### Rank 5
On aim: x0.2 explosion radius, +100% damage and +5.5% ammo efficiency for every 1m radius lost.

> [!TIP] Tip
> Ideally this is used in conjunction with [[Primed Firestorm]] on a weapon with large AoE for full efficacy.
