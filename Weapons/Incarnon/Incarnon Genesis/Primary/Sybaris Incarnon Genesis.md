# Sybaris Prime Builds

> [!TIP] Recommended Spliced Riven Configurations
> Sybaris Incarnon has **FORCED** Blast Status, so there's no reason to mod for additional Blast damage with elemental mods. Instead, focus on getting:
> - +Viral (Spliced from Cold + Toxin) OR +Status Damage (Spliced from Damage + Status Chance)
> - +Status Chance
> - +Cold for additional Critical Damage / +Heat for DoT with 50% Armor Reduction / Damage
> - -Zoom



## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Fire 4-round bursts and gain Blast Status.

### Evolution `II`: Blazing Barrel

- Increase Base Damage by +15
- On Firing: +5% Multishot. Stacks up to 10x.

### Evolution `III`: Marksman's Hand

-60% Weapon Recoil

### Evolution `IV`: Reaver's Rapture

On Full Burst Hit: +20% Damage
Resets on reload.

## Viral Riven

Spliced Rivens are now mandatory for dealing the most amount of damage. For example, Viral, while a good status effect requires two mod slots to create under normal circumstances.

> [!NOTICE]
> The stats below for this build are affected by [[Tenacious Bond]], which provides +1.2x Final Critical Damage multiplier. In this case, the Critical Damage multiplier of Sybaris Prime goes from 5.6x without a Companion to 6.8x with a Companion equipped with the Tenacious Bond mod active.
