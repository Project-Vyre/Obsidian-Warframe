# Stug Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> All hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below unless if otherwise stated. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Unleash a chaotic maelstrom of bouncing corrosive blobs.

### Evolution `II`: Blazing Barrel

- Increase Base Damage by +175
- On Firing:
    - +5% Multishot
    - Stacks up to 10x.
    - Resets on Reload.

### Evolution `III`: Resonant Restore

On Reload from Empty: Increase Base Magazine Capacity by +10. Stacks up to 3x.

### Evolution `IV`: Deathtrap Trigger

- Increase Base Critical Chance by 25%
- Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +1.5x
