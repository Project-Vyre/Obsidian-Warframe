[[Nidus#Virulence (1)|Virulence]] Augment

Hitting 4 enemies with Virulence grants 200% **Primary Weapon Critical Chance** for 15s.

> [!WARNING]
> Critical Chance caps out at 600% for **every weapon**. So even if you have an insane amount of Ability Strength and see 1000% Critical Chance, you will **not** benefit unless if your Primary Weapon's Riven has a negative Critical Chance to counteract.
