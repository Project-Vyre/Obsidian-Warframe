# Abilities

## Passive

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> 10% of Health Orbs are converted into Energy, and 10% of Energy Orbs are converted into Health.

## Metamorphosis (1)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Switch forms, temporarily gaining bonus Shields and Armor in Night-Form or bonus Damage and Speed in Day-Form.

## Rest & Rage (2)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> In Night-Form, targets are put to sleep. In Day-Form, targets become more vulnerable to damage.



### Rest & Rage Tips

- In addition to Day-Form causing targets to be more vulnerable to damage, it also speeds up enemies. This is commonly used to speed up the Hemocyte during Operation: Plaguestar.
- Damage Vulnerability stacks with Nova's [[Nova#Molecular Prime (4)]]

## Pacify & Provoke (3)

> [!QUOTE]
> In Night-Form, reduces damage inflicted by nearby enemies.
> In Day-Form, increases Ability Strength of nearby allies.

> [!WARNING]
> The Ability Strength granted has a **hard cap** of 50%.
