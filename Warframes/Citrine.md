# Abilities

## Passive

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Citrine grants nearby allies 5 health regeneration per second. Pick up a Health Orb to increase regeneration by 0.1, up to a maximum of 25.

## Fractured Blast (1)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Slash and stagger enemies with a crystal blast that inflicts Slash Status. Enemies afflicted with this Status Effect have an increased chance of dropping Health and Energy Orbs.

### Fractured Blast Tips

- While Prismatic Gem is active, Fractured Blast is an easy way to inflict additional Status Effects and obtain both Health and Energy Orbs once the enemies are dead.

## Preserving Shell (2)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Citrine guards herself and nearby allies with a crystalline shell that gradually decays. Kills and assists increase the defensive power of the shell.

### Preserving Shell Tips

- Ability Strength affects the **starting** Damage Reduction.
- At Rank 3, a minimum of 225% Ability Strength sets the initial Damage Reduction to 90%.

## Prismatic Gem (3)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Deploy a gem that shoots prismatic beams. The gem targets enemies that are taking weapon damage from Citrine and her allies. Its beams inflict Heat, Cold, Toxin and Electricity Status Effects. Status Chance and Status Duration increase for nearby allies.

### Prismatic Gem Tips

- In the worst case scenario where you don't have any ammo for your ranged weapons, you can just pull out your Fortuna Mining Laser and kill an Acolyte with it in Steel Path.

## Crystallize (4)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Citrine summons crystal fractals. The fractals rush forward, seeking enemies. Enemies touched by the fractals are paralyzed by crystalline growths. Hit the growths to deal Critical Damage.

### Crystallize Tips

> [!FAIL] Warning
> Crystallize **sets** Critical Chance to 300%. It **does not** increase Critical Chance by 300%. This means if your weapon has Critical Chance higher than 300%, it will be immediately set back to 300% when shooting Citrine's crystals.
