# Abilities

## Passive

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Banshee pierces the ears of enemies within a 20m radius when casting Abilities, applying max Puncture Status Effect. Additionally, Banshee's weapon noise is hushed.

Put simply, Banshee's passive makes it so that enemies do **80% less damage at all times** within 20m as you are always casting Banshee's abilities.

**All weapons, including those from companions and robotic compansions are silenced.**

## Sonic Boom (1)

Base stats:
- Drain: 25
- Range: 15m

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Banshee emits a sonic shockwave that pushes targets in range with enough force to incapacitate attackers and reduce their Armor.

Post rework Banshee as of the Iceblade of Narin update, this ability now strips armor permanently by itself without its augment.

>[!TIP] What is the Push Force stat?
>The `Push Force` is measured in meters per second that the enemy is pushed away from you.

### Sonic Boom Tips

- Can be used to push back Demolishers in Disruption missions for easy dispatching. I've personally pushed many Demolishers *and* Acolytes into the corner where they are immediately helpless. Push Force is **not** nullified by Demolishers.
- Forced Impact Status Effect makes priority targets vulnerable to Mercy kills.

## Sonar (2)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Using acoustic location, Banshee's Sonar power finds and tracks enemies, and exposes critical weak points to everyone in your squad.

Weak points created by Banshee on enemy body parts use the usual body damage multiplier instead of the usual headshot multiplier. **However**, the weakpoints use the damage multiplier of whatever you have modded based on Banshee's ability strength.

> [!WARNING] About Banshee's generated Weak Points on the enemy body...
> Weak points on the body use the body damage multiplier, **not** the usual Headshot damage multiplier of 3x.
### Sonar Tips

- Highlighted weak points from recasting Sonar or [[Resonance]] can overlap, increasing the Damage Multiplier.
- Weak points created by Sonar are treated the same as regular enemy weak points, meaning they can charge Incarnon weapons, and trigger other abilities related to weak points. Weapons can also be modded for additional weak point damage.

## Silence (3)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Using Silence surrounds Banshee in an aura that stuns enemies, limits their perception and tactical response to gunfire and Warframe attacks, and renders them unable to activate their abilities.

This ability **disables** the majority of Eximus abilities with the main exception being Entropic Eximus and their usually invulnerable Kuva bubble. Please see the official Warframe Wiki page here for additional details: [Silence - WARFRAME Wiki](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Silence)

### Silence Tips

- Recasting Silence while active will stun enemies who are already in range of the effect while also resetting the ability's duration.

## Sound Quake (4)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Channeling all of her acoustic energy into the environment, Banshee uses ultrasonic reverberations to violently shake the ground in a targeted area.

This ability without the augment Gaseous Quake is mainly used for the following:
- Constant Crowd Control to both stagger *and* push away enemies from an area you want to defend.
- Applying [[Blast]] status effects.

The reasoning behind this build is due to Banshee's still poor general survivability and self-sustain when playing solo even post-rework after the Iceblade of Narin update, so allow me to write my thought process.

### Why Arcane Circumvent?

Arcane Circumvent is essentially [[Catalyzing Shields]] (in the form of immediately getting Overshield) and [[Rolling Guard]] (player Overguard **negates** all Status Effects) condensed into an Arcane package with **no cooldown**. 

The only glaring issues with this Arcane are:
- The enemies you are fighting against dictates what defenses you will get.
- Reliant on Eximus presence to provide Overguard.
- Your teammates killing the enemies you were trying to gain defenses from. The enemies may also have their defenses removed by other players before you even have the chance to roll into them.

The Overguard provided also counts as *ally* Overguard, which then ties into Rage giving you energy in the situations that enemy density is too low to make use of Equilibrium or lack a support frame like Citrine (Fractured Blast) or Follie (Passive) who can naturally increase the drop rate of Health *and* Energy Orbs with their respective abilities. Warframes that can make full utilization of [[Arcane Universal Fallout]] also provide easy but substantial sustain.

### Why Adaptation?

It is one of two reasons that Banshee does not *immediately* get deleted, with the other reason being her Passive. However, in the case that Puncture Status is not active on the surrounding enemies Adaptation is your saving grace. Here's where [[Sonic Siphon]] also helps Adaptation become more effective as it gives 1,500 Armor to Banshee which can be refreshed constantly, even on enemies without Armor.

> [!WARNING] But... Slash damage...
> Slash damage gets mitigated by your Shields, so you will be fine in the majority of situations.



> [!FAIL] But... Toxin damage.
> Toxin damage in situations like a Tank Assassination with both environmental Toxin damage and enemies (including the tank) dealing high amounts of Toxin in 1999 in the Elite version of Temporal Archimedea pose a problem in survivability as Shield gating will not protect you due to the nature of Toxin bypassing that mechanic.

...the issue with Toxin damage leads us to...

### Why Molt Reconstruct and Primed Vigor?

- Primed Vigor increases max Health to reduce the effectiveness of Toxin damage so you can at least survive 645 Toxin damage as the next tick of Toxin is not immediate.
    - More Shield to absorb more damage that can tank more damage with the help of Adaptation.
- Primed Vigor, Adaptation, [[Sonic Siphon]] and Banshee's Passive all work together to make you tank large amounts of damage that other Warframes can't exactly tank easily.

With the energy gained from Rage / Hunter Adrenaline after your Shields go down or from damage inflicted on the Overguard you gained from [[Arcane Circumvent]], you can **self heal** and survive Toxin DoT which is most likely already reduced from Banshee's Passive. This uniquely makes her able to tank damage without having complete Status immunity and a Channeled Ability (an ability that is toggled on / off and continuously drains Energy.)
