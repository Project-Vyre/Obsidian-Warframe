# Abilities

## Passive

> [!QUOTE] In-Game Tool Tip
> Uriel commands, empowers, and protects three unique demons. 
> 
> CATENACH: Snares and slows enemies chaining them together. Damage dealt to any target in the chain is inflicted on all shared enemies. (Unlocks with Infernalis)
> 
> GULPHAGOR: Latches onto foes dealing damage over time. If killed, a circle of pain is spawned. (Unlocks with Remedium)
> 
> VYTHELAS: Inscribes demonic runes upon fallen foes that add Fire Rate and Heat damage when collected. (Unlocks with Demonium)

All three demons are **autonomous and fight with you** as you progress through your favorite tileset. Be careful though, as they can die and have a **60 second respawn timer** unless revived manually with Uriel's 2, **Remedium**.

## Infernalis (1)

Base stats:
- Drain: 25
- Duration: 35s
- Radius: 2m
- Damage: 2
- Damage/Second: 1500
  
**CATENACH:**
- Duration: 10s
- Damage/Second: 100
- Slow: 50%

> [!QUOTE] In-Game Tool Tip
> Manifest an aura of flames inflicting Heat Damage and Status Effect on nearby enemies. 
> Tap Dodge while airborne to take flight as a flaming meteor of destruction.

### Infernalis Tips

- Can be used as a movement tool if you have issues with moving from objective to objective at a fast pace.
- Can be cancelled by holding the button, shooting, aiming, hitting walls, etc.
- Inflicts the status effect of Heat on enemies, causing them to panic and reducing the incoming damage.
- Speed is effected by parkour velocity mods.
- The demons are also affected by Infernalis, therefore casting Infernalis and following it up with Demonium allows the demons to carry the Infernalis aura towards enemies. 

> [!WARNING] Known Problems with Infernalis...
> 
> Nullifier bubbles and basically any tileset that has a bunch of corridors can drive you crazy if you use Infernalis a lot to roam around the map.
> 
> Charges his final ability really slowly unless paired with the new augment Infernum.

## Remedium (2)

Base Stats:
- Drain: 50
- Heal: 50%
  
**GULPHAGOR:**
- Latch Duration: 10s
- Effect Duration: 10s
- Radius: 4m
- Damage: 750
- Damage/Second: 200
- Health Orb Drop: 300%
- Energy Orb Drop: 100%

> [!QUOTE] In-Game Tool Tip
> Uriel heals himself and his demons. If his demons are dead, they are resurrected.

### Remedium Tips

- Using the ability whilst Uriel is at max Health can also restore his Shields. This includes Overshields.
- Uriel can use the ability whenever he wants, as long as he has the energy to do it. The Shield restoration at max Health also works if the demons are dead, double dipping on the ability's effect.
- The orbs dropped from the enemy Gulphagor latched onto can trigger mods like Equilibrium as well as arcanes like Arcane Energize, however both are unnecessary to equip if kills are frequently made.

> [!WARNING] The Demons are a bit... squishy...
> In Steel Path where enemy density is higher and deadlier, you may run into issues where repeated casts of Remedium are required.

## Demonium (3)

Base Stats: 
- Drain: 75
- Damage: 250
- Duration: 5s
- Explosion Radius: 6m
- Damage Vulnerability: 50%
- Demon Health Drain: 10%

**VYTHELAS:**
- Fire Rate: 30%
- Extra Damage: 30%
- Buff Duration: 10s

> [!QUOTE] In-Game Tool Tip
> Uriel rips out the souls of his demons, draining their health, and sends them in search of new victims. Souls explode on contact with an enemy, rending it vulnerable to Damage.

### Demonium Tips

- As stated above, using this ability after casting Infernalis allows the demons to carry the aura to enemies. 
- Your allies are capable of picking up the runes generated by Vythelas, giving them the same benefits as you without the added part of generating Brimstone progress.

> [!WARNING] Energy problems!
> Without use of Uriel's new augment, Demonium is arguably the best way of generating the most progress on Brimstone. 
> 
> With a poorly modded Uriel, you will eventually run into energy issues with repeated casts of 1 and 3 with the occasional 2 in the middle unless you have passable efficiency or other ways of gaining more energy. 
> 
> Magentic procs or other ways the game reduces your energy will actively handicap your gameplay loop.


## Brimstone (4)

Base Stats:
- Drain: 75
- Duration: 10s
- Radius: 15m
- Damage/Second: 1500
- Brimstone Multiplier Cap: 10x

> [!QUOTE] In-Game Tool Tip
> Uriel and his demons create a growing ring of flaming brimstone. Charge the ability by utilizing demon abilities. Damage inflicted increases with each consecutive hit. 

### Brimstone Tips

- Modding for Duration, Range, and Strength can make Brimstone last longer and hit harder whilst also covering more ground.   
  
- Using Brimstone again whilst another is active won't overwrite the active zone, it'll simply create a new one to act alongside the current one. 
  
- Casting Brimstone against high health enemies like Acolytes or mini-bosses can shred their health bar.
  
- Brimstone is a great tool to use during the rollover portion of a fissure. Simply wait for your reactant to reset at 0, then drop Brimstone for free drops during the countdown timer. 

> [!WARNING] Recharged ability!
> As stated, Brimstone can only be used once the meter is full. Not sooner.
> 
> Once used, you'll need to build the meter up again. Uriel's newest augment has made this easier.

# Builds
These are the most common builds for Uriel, taken from various sources.

## "Hellfire!" 

This build is taken from MrGodofWar7, an active user of Uriel. 

This is his **primary** build, consisting of high Range with a decent amount of Duration and Strength. He often finds himself swapping between Arcane Hot Shot and Arcane Concentration, wishing you could equip 3 Arcanes instead of just 2. Molt Efficiency is also equipped for extra Ability Duration since Uriel is constantly capable of restoring his own Shields.
 
Arcanes:
- Arcane Concentration/Arcane Hot Shot
- Molt Efficiency

Operator School: Zenurik/Any

Archon Shards:
- Tauforged Crimson: 15% Ability Strength
- Tauforged Crimson: 15% Ability Duration
- Tauforged Amber: 37.5% Casting Speed
- Tauforged Crimson: 15% Ability Duration
- Tauforged Azure: 75% Energy Max

|                      | Growing Power    | Power Drift/Cunning Drift |                 |
| -------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------- | --------------- |
| Blind Rage           | Primed Flow      | Primed Continuity         | Overextended    |
| Augur Reach/Infernum | Stretch/Infernum | Equilibrium               | Archon Vitality |
Once Infernum is acquired from either Steel Meridian or The Perrin Sequence, swap out either Augur Reach or Stretch depending on where the Forma is placed for the Naramon polarity.

If you do not own the augment yet, keep both Auger Reach and Stretch on for the extra range. 

### Why choose between Arcane Concentration and Arcane Hot Shot?

Ask yourself whether or not you want more Critical Chance on your weapons after using an ability that deals Heat damage or an extra 60% Duration for 3 seconds after using an ability and make your choice. It's simply personal preference.

### Why is the Efficiency so low??

The energy collected from Vythelas is more than enough to counteract the low efficiency.

### Is Archon Vitality that important?

Considering the secondary effect is applying the Heat damage twice from your abilities is there... yes. Yes it is.
