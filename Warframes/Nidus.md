> Nidus is for all intents and purposes... practically unkillable.

# Abilities

## Passive

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> If Nidus is killed with at least 15 stacks of Mutation, those 15 stacks are consumed; this grants 5s of invulnerability and restores Health to 50%.

## Virulence (1)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Rupture the ground with a damaging fungal growth that steals energy from each enemy it strikes. For every five enemies hit, the Infestation mutates, multiplying its destructive force.

Virulence is *mostly* used as a utility ability to obtain Mutation stacks, but can kill enemies up to a certain point the more Mutation stacks you have.

Augment(s): [[Teeming Virulence]]
## Larva (2)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Spawn an Infested pod that erupts with tendrils, latches onto nearby enemies and pulls them in. Enemies killed while held have a chance of generating a Mutation stack.

Larva is used as a utility ability to bunch up enemies to make it easier to obtain Mutation stacks when used with Virulence.

## Parasitic Link (3)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Bind to a target with parasitic link. When cast on an ally, both the host and Nidus have their Ability Strength and Weapon Damage increased. Linked enemies take some of the damage inflicted on Nidus.

This ability can be used to give a substantial Ability Strength increase to allies and can result in very beneficial interactions.

Examples include:
- Need even higher Damage Multipliers from [[Banshee]]? Nidus has you covered.
- Linking up with [[Sirius and Orion|Sirius]] before casting [[Sirius and Orion#Light's Sanctuary (S3)|Light's Sanctuary]] provides 100% Damage Reduction.

Augments: [[Parasitic Vitality]]

## Ravenous (4)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Gluttonous maggots swarm nearby enemies, feasting until they are hit with [[#Virulence (1)]] and burst with Infestation. The maggots benefit from Mutation and each enemy hit adds to the Mutation stack.

Augment(s): [[Insatiable]]

### Why not Abundant Mutation?

While it increases the maximum amount of Mutation stacks that Nidus can hold, it also adds a 30 second **cooldown** to Undying. If Nidus reaches 0 Health **during** the 30 seconds cooldown period, Nidus will go into **Bleedout** AND lose **ALL STACKS OF MUTATION**.

This augment essentially makes Nidus pathetically squishy and easily killable and is why I will never use this augment.
