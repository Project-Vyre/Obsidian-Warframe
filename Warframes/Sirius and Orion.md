## Light's Sanctuary (S3)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Sirius creates a well of light that heals and revives allies, while reducing incoming damage. The well slowly grows in size and power.
> 
> (HOLD) Swap to Orion and use Event Horizon.

> [!TIP] Damage Reduction Soft Cap
> Light's Sanctuary has a **soft cap** of 75% Damage Reduction. However, additional Ability Strength can push it to 100% Damage Reduction and beyond. This is achieved with a minimum of 667% Ability Strength which can be obtained with the help of: [[Nidus#Parasitic Link (3)|Nidus's Parasitic Link]], [[Nokko#Brightbonnet (2)|Nokko's Brightbonnet]], [[Equinox#Pacify & Provoke (3)|Equinox's Pacify & Provoke in Day Form]] or Madurai's Power Strike. 5 Tauforged Crimson Shards may also be allocated to 15% Ability Strength.
